# Energy Efficiency Prediction

This project focuses on predicting the energy efficiency of buildings based on various architectural and structural features. 

## Dataset
The dataset (`energy_efficiency_data.csv`) includes features such as relative compactness, surface area, wall area, roof area, overall height, orientation, glazing area, and glazing area distribution. 

## Objective
To develop a predictive model that can accurately estimate the heating load and cooling load requirements of buildings, thus aiding in architectural design and energy conservation efforts.

## Methodology
The analysis is conducted in `Energy Efficiency.ipynb` and involves data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, and the application of regression models to predict energy efficiency metrics.
